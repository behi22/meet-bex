What Bex can do

Say “Hey Bex” and ask in your own words. Every screen here is from the demo.

Draw and code live

Websites, posters and moving art, written live while you watch. Change them by voice, make them 3D, go back to any version.

Build an AI receptionist

A real voice agent for your business, built in seconds. Change it by voice, then talk to it yourself.

Talk naturally

She answers out loud and keeps the conversation going, even through pauses.

Make pictures

Describe anything and she makes a picture of it on the spot.

Test it on a live call

Bex calls the agent she built, books a time while you listen, and reviews the call.

Play with your voice

Your drawing becomes the player, and you steer it with your voice.

Find things out

Live answers and research with sources, laid out while she talks.

Know where you are

The place on a map, the local time and the weather, all live.

Take it home

When you say goodbye, a QR code opens your own visit pass on your phone, with everything you made.
