Voice AI · Live demo

Meet Bex

A voice AI host you just talk to. While she answers, everything she says shows up on screen, and she can look things up, make things and even build other AI agents for you on the spot.

What Bex can do

Say "Hey Bex" and ask for any of it, in your own words.

Talk naturally

She answers out loud, keeps the conversation going and picks up right where you left off.

Find things out

Live research with sources, shown on a card while she talks you through it.

Weather and places

The forecast, the time, and where you are on a globe, all live.

Make pictures

Describe something and she makes an image of it on the spot.

Draw and code live

Moving art, websites and posters, written piece by piece while you watch. Change them by voice, make them 3D, go back to any version.

Play with your voice

Your drawing becomes the player in a game you steer by saying left and right, or by humming to fly.

Build an AI receptionist

A real voice agent for your business, built in seconds, then she puts you through to it so you can talk to it yourself.

Test it on a live call

Bex calls the agent she built, books an appointment with it while you listen, then reviews how it did.

Take it home

When you say goodbye, a QR code opens your own visit pass on your phone, with everything you made.

Built by Behbod Babai, on his own, on the Alebex voice engine, using only its public developer docs and APIs.
